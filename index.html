Staff

              
People listed here can sign in and use the Income and Payments tabs. They see unpaid orders and the last few days of entries, never the report or the full history. Removing someone blocks them straight away.

              

                
                
                
                
              

              
This creates their login. Give them the email and password; they can change the password later with “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

              

            

            

              
Backup

              
All records are stored online in your Firebase database. Download a backup now and then to keep your own copy. A backup from the old offline version can be uploaded here.
